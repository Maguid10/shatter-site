Frequently asked questions

How does the bottle work?

Every day starts with a full bottle. When you log a drink, it empties by that amount. At midnight it’s full again. Your alcohol-free days, money saved and badges keep counting in the background.

I had a drink. Do I lose everything?

No. Logging a drink starts a new alcohol-free run, but your total days, check-ins, savings and badges stay saved. Honesty is part of the progress.

How do I change my drink, goal or start date?

Open Settings (the gear on the home screen). You can pick a bottle, can, wine, cocktail or shot, set a goal, change your alcohol-free start date and switch between oz and ml.

I’m not getting reminders.

Check that Daily check-in is on in the app’s Settings, and that notifications for Shatter are allowed in your iPhone’s Settings → Notifications.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Shatter and tap Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or period ends to avoid being charged.

I got a new phone. How do I restore my purchase?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open the paywall in Shatter and tap Restore.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and choose Shatter.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Reset everything, or delete the app. Your data only lives on your device, so that removes it completely.

Need more help? If you’re struggling with alcohol, you don’t have to do it alone. In the US you can call the SAMHSA National Helpline at 1-800-662-4357 (free, 24/7). In the UK, see NHS alcohol support.

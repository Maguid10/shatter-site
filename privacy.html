Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

The short version: Shatter has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your drinks, check-ins and answers stay on your iPhone. We never sell your data.

What the app stores

To work, Shatter saves the following only on your device:

  • Your onboarding answers: age range, goal, what you drink, drinks per week, price per drink, currency, your reasons and your last drink date.
  • Drinks you log, daily check-ins and moods, your streaks, goals and settings.

This data is not sent to us or to any server. We cannot see it.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your iPhone as local notifications. No notification data leaves your device.

Subscriptions

Payments are handled by Apple through your Apple ID; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase details (such as plan and renewal date), never your drinking data. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Links to other sites

The app links to health sources such as the NHS and NIAAA. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings → Reset everything in the app, or delete the app. Because your data only lives on your device, that removes it completely.

Age

Shatter is intended for adults of legal drinking age in their country. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
